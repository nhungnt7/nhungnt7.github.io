# About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Data Science & AI at [Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/) in Melbourne, Australia. I am supervised by [Prof. Dinh Phung](https://dinhphung.ml/), [Prof. Thuy-Trang Vu](https://trangvu.github.io/), and [Dr. Linhao Luo](https://rmanluo.github.io/).

I have more than five years of experience spanning applied machine learning research and production AI. I build and evaluate LLM and agentic systems, with research spanning multi-agent safety, autonomy, and robustness, alignment, long-horizon planning, fine-tuning, and automated evaluation. My work has been published at EMNLP, COLM, CoNLL, NAACL, and LREC-COLING, and I previously led LLM and retrieval-augmented generation products from experimentation to deployment.

My research aims to advance agentic systems and frontier models for planning and reasoning, and to enable large-scale coordination through agentic collectives and economies. This includes intelligent orchestration and communication, self-improvement, and agentic safety and autonomy.

# Research

My current research focuses on safe and autonomous multi-agent systems, including:

- LLM alignment and truth consistency
- Long-horizon planning and reasoning
- Intelligent orchestration and inter-agent communication
- Self-improvement, safety evaluation, and robustness
- Retrieval-augmented generation and table understanding
- Emergent bias in multi-agent systems

**Technical expertise:** Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, FastAPI, Rasa, LangChain, LangGraph, LLM fine-tuning and alignment, reinforcement learning, RAG, hybrid search, Text-to-SQL, Text-to-Cypher, Elasticsearch, Qdrant, Neo4j, MongoDB, and Docker.
